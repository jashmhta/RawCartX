Get to know the RawKart story.

Our Story

RawKart was founded on a simple, yet powerful idea: the food industry deserves a supplier that is as committed to purity and transparency as they are. We saw a gap between the producers of exceptional raw ingredients and the businesses striving to create clean, high-quality products for consumers. Our journey began with a mission to bridge that gap, building a supply chain founded on trust, quality, and partnership.

From day one, our focus has been on sourcing the finest ingredients directly from their origins, ensuring they are natural and minimally processed. We believe that what we leave out is just as important as what we put in. This philosophy is the bedrock of our company and the promise we make to every client.

Our Journey

2004 - The Spark

RawKart is founded by a team of food scientists and supply chain experts with a vision to revolutionize ingredient sourcing.

2008 - First Warehouse

Opened our first state-of-the-art warehouse, setting new standards for ingredient storage and handling.

2012 - Global Sourcing Initiative

Expanded our network to over 15 countries, establishing direct partnerships with growers and producers worldwide.

2018 - Tech Integration

Launched our proprietary traceability platform, offering clients unprecedented transparency into the supply chain.

2024 - A New Era

Celebrating 20 years of excellence, RawKart continues to lead the industry in quality, innovation, and reliability.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To be the most reliable B2B source for pure, high-quality food ingredients, empowering our partners to create exceptional products that contribute to a healthier world. We are dedicated to a transparent supply chain, rigorous quality standards, and building lasting relationships.

Our Vision: To lead the food ingredient industry towards a future where authenticity and transparency are the norm. We envision a world where every business, big or small, has access to ingredients they can trust, enabling innovation and fostering consumer confidence.

Raw. Real. Reliable.

Our tagline is more than just words; it's our operating principle and the core of our identity.

  • Raw: We honor the natural integrity of our ingredients. We source materials that are as close to their original state as possible, ensuring purity and potency.
  • Real: We operate with genuine purpose and authenticity. Our mission is real, our partnerships are real, and our commitment to our customers' success is unwavering.
  • Reliable: We are the partner you can depend on. From consistent quality and supply to dependable service and expert support, reliability is woven into every aspect of our business.
